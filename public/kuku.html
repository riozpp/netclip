FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms are supported?

TikTok, REDnote (Xiaohongshu), Douyin, Bilibili and Instagram public posts. Private, followers-only and paid content are out of scope because they need an authenticated session.

Is it really free?

Yes. Each IP gets 60 parses per rolling hour, which covers normal personal use. There is no paywall and no sign-up step in this build.

Why do I sometimes see a rate-limit message?

The quota exists to keep the service usable for everyone. It refills automatically; the message tells you exactly when.

Do you support batch downloads?

Yes. Paste up to six links, one per line. They are resolved in parallel and shown as separate result cards, each with its own download buttons.

What if a link fails to parse?

Most failures come from truncated links or deleted posts. The parser tries several strategies per platform, so a transient platform change usually recovers within minutes — just retry.

Where do the files go?

Directly to your device. Our proxy adds the referer header the CDN expects and sets an attachment filename, then streams the bytes through without writing them anywhere.
